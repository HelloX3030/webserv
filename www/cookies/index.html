Browser flow

  1. Step 1: set cookies
  2. Step 2: show cookies sent by browser

curl flow

curl -i -c /tmp/webserv-cookies.txt http://127.0.0.1:8080/cgi-bin/set-cookies.py
curl -i -b /tmp/webserv-cookies.txt http://127.0.0.1:8080/cgi-bin/show-cookies.py
